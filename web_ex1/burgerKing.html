About Us

Burger King, founded in 1954, is the second-largest fast-food hamburger chain globally, known for its flame-grilled burgers and the "Home of the Whopper". With over 18,700 locations in more than 100 countries, Burger King is a well-recognized brand with a focus on quality ingredients, signature recipes, and family-friendly dining experiences. The vast majority of Burger King restaurants are independently owned and operated by franchisees.
